Between the mountains and the Mediterranean.

Set above the coast of Deià, Casa Oliva is a home in conversation with its surroundings. Local stone, warm oak and generous glazing draw the landscape inside, while deep overhangs temper the Mediterranean light.

Four en-suite bedrooms open to private terraces. At the heart of the house, an open-plan living space extends towards the infinity pool and uninterrupted sea views. Every detail is designed for a slower, more considered way of living.
